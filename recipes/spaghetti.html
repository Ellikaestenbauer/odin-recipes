Spaghetti



Description

Spaghetti is a classic Italian pasta dish made with long, thin noodles tossed in a savory sauce. It's a simple yet satisfying meal that's perfect for a quick dinner or a casual outing.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  2. In a pan, heat the olive oil and sauté the minced garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the tomato sauce and let it simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Drain the cooked spaghetti and add it to the sauce.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  6. Serve hot with grated parmesan cheese on top. Enjoy your spaghetti!
