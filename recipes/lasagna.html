Lasagna



Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese. It's a hearty and comforting meal that's perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 500g minced meat
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 400g canned tomatoes
  • 200g lasagna sheets
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 100g parmesan
  • Salt and pepper
  • Olive oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. In a pan, heat olive oil and sauté the chopped onion and garlic until translucent.
  3. Add the minced meat and cook until browned. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Add the canned tomatoes and let it simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. In a baking dish, layer the meat sauce, lasagna sheets, and mozzarella. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a layer of meat sauce and parmesan on top.
  6. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Then remove the foil and bake for an additional 10 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
  7. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving. Enjoy your lasagna!
