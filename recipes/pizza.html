Pizza



Description

Pizza is a beloved Italian dish consisting of a flattened round base of leavened wheat-based dough topped with tomatoes, cheese, and various other ingredients. It's a versatile and delicious meal that's perfect for casual dining or social gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 200g mozzarella
  • 100g parmesan
  • Various toppings (e.g., pepperoni, mushrooms, bell peppers)
  • Olive oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Sprinkle the mozzarella and parmesan on top.
  5. Add your desired toppings.
  6. Bake for 10-15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  7. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving. Enjoy your pizza!
